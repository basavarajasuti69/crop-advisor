## Rice - Healthy
Rice plants with uniform green color and no spots on leaves are healthy.
No treatment needed. Continue standard irrigation and fertilization.

## Rice - Leaf Blight
Leaf blight shows long tan or white lesions with reddish-brown margins on rice leaves.
Remove affected leaves. Avoid excess nitrogen. Apply an approved fungicide as per label directions.
Confirm with your local agriculture officer before spraying.

## Wheat - Healthy
Wheat with upright green stems and no yellow streaks or rust pustules is healthy.

## Wheat - Rust
Wheat rust appears as orange-brown pustules on leaves and stems.
Use resistant varieties and apply fungicide as per label directions.
Confirm with your local agriculture officer before spraying.

## Cotton - Healthy
Cotton with green leaves and no curling or discoloration is healthy.

## Cotton - Leaf Curl Virus
Leaf curl causes upward curling, thickening, and vein swelling of cotton leaves.
Control whiteflies. Remove infected plants. There is no cure once infected.
Confirm with your local agriculture officer before spraying.

## Maize - Healthy
Maize with broad green leaves and no lesions is healthy.

## Maize - Leaf Blight
Maize leaf blight shows long elliptical tan lesions on leaves.
Rotate crops and apply fungicide as per label directions.
Confirm with your local agriculture officer before spraying.

## Tomato - Healthy
Tomato plants with green leaves and no spots or wilting are healthy.

## Tomato - Blight
Tomato blight shows dark water-soaked spots on leaves and fruit.
Remove affected leaves. Apply fungicide as per label directions.
Confirm with your local agriculture officer before spraying.
